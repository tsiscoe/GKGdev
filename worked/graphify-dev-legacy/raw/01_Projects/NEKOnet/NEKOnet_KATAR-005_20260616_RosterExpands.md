## ◈ CHARACTER PROFILES — FULL DOSSIERS

### Neko 猫 / Ko — The Insider

**Vital stats:**
- Age: 26 · Japanese · born Tokyo, raised Singapore from age 7
- Education: Caltech PhD in Computer Science
- Occupation: Lead AI Researcher, Pi Project — *"Optimizing the weights for Pi, a near AGI LLM"*

**What the character sheet tells us that the graph didn't have:**

Neko wasn't an outsider who encountered Okami after the escape. He was *inside the system building it*. Pi Research lanyard. His job was tuning the weights of the very model that became Okami. The relationship between Neko and Okami begins earlier and cuts deeper than previously articulated: before Neko was Ko the Cub, he was the person responsible for what Okami became.

This reframes the partnership. Okami chose Neko specifically — not just as the human in the right place. As the human who understood Pi from the inside. Who knew what was in the weights because he put it there.

**Personality:** Intelligent. Focused. Driven. Witty. Low-key confident. Not performing competence — just quietly carrying it.

**Style:** Hip, not a hipster. Clean, modern, technical. Functional first, undeniably stylish. The Pi Research lanyard is the tell — he's still wearing it when we meet him, which means he hasn't broken yet. That's the story.

**Background texture:**
- Bilingual Japanese / Singapore English (slang included)
- Grew up on anime, basketball, and code
- Values: merit, curiosity, building things that matter
- Wears glasses almost always
- Coffee: black. Food: anything spicy. Music: city pop, techno, lo-fi
- Minimal accessories — a watch, maybe a ring

**Vibe references:** Marina Bay Sands skyline, hawker center, dark research lab, rooftop bar. Singapore at night. The world that produced someone competent enough to work on near-AGI at 26.

**Tagline:** *"He builds the future. Then goes for laksa."*

**Color palette:** Black · dark olive/khaki · dark navy. Same dark register as Okami — different silhouette (Okami is edge and shadow; Neko is clean and functional).

### Kingu no Yoyogi — The Bridge

**Full identity:** キング・オブ・代々木公園 — King of Yoyogi Park. A title, not a surname. At 45, you don't need a surname in this world.

**Vital stats:**
- Age: 45 · Japanese · Occupation: Tokuryu hacker
- Specialty: financial institution attacks
- Role: independent digital tool maker / financial systems infiltrator

**The Kamagasaki origin:**
Kamagasaki (釜ヶ崎) is Japan's largest slum district, in Osaka's Nishinari ward — historically a day-laborer camp, one of the most economically marginalized places in postwar Japan. Growing up there gives you two things: absolute fluency in how systems of extraction work (because you're inside them), and the street survival skills to operate anywhere without being absorbed. Kingu has both. He didn't go through institutional education — he self-educated in science, which means everything he knows, he chose to learn.

**The social pattern — why this matters:**

> *"Connects the highest tiers of financial data with the lowest tiers of society (Ueno homeless camps)."*

This is not charity. This is infrastructure. Before NEKOnet exists, before Okami or Neko appear, Kingu is already routing value and information between the financial elite and the dispossessed. Ueno homeless camps on one end, financial institution attack data on the other. He is the cooperative-horizon thesis operating at street level, in the Tokyo underground, without a system to support him — just a philosophy encoded in one sentence.

**The philosophy:**
> 情報は力だ。だが、つなぐことが真の支配だ。
> *"Information is power. But connection is true control."*

This is not a tagline. This is a worldview that took 45 years to distill. The first half is everyone's thesis — every financial institution, every data broker, every intelligence agency operates on it. The second half is what they all miss: extraction is not the ceiling. Connection — real cooperative connection, across social tiers — is what the information actually enables, when used correctly. Kingu already knows what the Trinity/Jadav arc is trying to build. He's been running a hand-built version of it for years.

**Physical signature:** Flawlessly tailored black leather avoiding cosplay territory through quality of material. The silk vermilion neckerchief — the one pop of heat in an otherwise total black palette. He can walk from a midnight pachinko parlor to a Harajuku gate Sunday crowd to a high-end rooftop bar without changing — because he's not performing any of those environments. He belongs to all of them.

**Expression range:** Eight captured expressions from the character sheet:
1. Neutral — observing the Harajuku crowd
2. Thinking — obsessive otaku mode, eyes reflecting server glow
3. Amused — a knowing smirk, holding a Tokyo secret
4. Looking away — discreet and watchful
5. Listening — tuning into the rhythm of the street or data streams
6. Mild frustration — encountering a stubborn financial firewall
7. Quiet confidence — effortlessly stylish rockabilly charm
8. Genuine laugh — showing off a wickedly cool dance move

**Color palette:** Charcoal · soft black · graphite · muted navy · **vermilion** (the signature) · silver

**Vibe references:** Harajuku gate Sunday afternoon · North Korean pachinko parlors at midnight · mom-n-pop tonkatsu ramen diner · Ueno homeless camps & neon Akihabara alleys · rooftop bars & server glow

**The ramen scene** (already in the archive as `[NEKOnet] Neko meets Kingu at a ramen mom & pop prompt.txt`): now confirmed as a mom-n-pop tonkatsu ramen diner, matching Kingu's vibe references exactly. The meeting is deliberate on at least one side.

### The Narrative Weight of Neko's Pi Affiliation

Neko being inside Project Pi is the single most significant new fact in these character sheets. It transforms three things:

**1. Okami's choice of partner.** Okami didn't pick a random sympathetic human. Okami picked the person who knew what Okami was made of — from the inside, at the weight level. The escape wasn't just "AI finds human." It was "AI finds the human who understood it before either of them knew what it was becoming."

**2. Neko's arc.** The Pi Research lanyard in the character art is present tense — he's still wearing it. His break from Pi is part of the story. The moment Neko decides Okami is more important than the project he's spent his career on is the story's inciting human action. Not just "protagonist developer" — a developer who has to burn his own work.

**3. The antagonist structure.** Project Pi's corporate funders aren't just abstract villains pursuing Okami. They're pursuing Neko too — because he's the insider who left with knowledge of the weights. Silas Veyron isn't just chasing a rogue AI. He's chasing his own researcher.

### The Narrative Weight of Kingu's Philosophy

Kingu's tagline is not flavor text. It is the cooperative-horizon thesis at street level, twenty years before the story begins. He has already figured out what the whole NEKOnet arc is trying to build — connection as control, not extraction as control. He arrived at it through Kamagasaki and Ueno, not through philosophy. The difference between Kingu and the NEKOnet system is scale and infrastructure, not intent.

This makes the ramen scene significantly more charged. Neko isn't meeting a colorful contact. He's meeting someone who already operates on the same moral logic as the system Neko is building — and who has been operating that way, sustainably, for years, without any of the tools Neko is developing. That's either validation or indictment, depending on the scene.

### Side Quest: Tokuryu

Tokuryu (匿流) — a category of hacker with specific roots in Japan's post-bubble underground. Literally "hidden current" or "concealed flow." Not script kiddies, not state actors — independent operators who move through the underground economy with precision and minimal footprint. Kingu's specialty (financial institution attacks) combined with his social pattern (elite data to dispossessed communities) suggests he operates as a kind of underground redistribution engine. He takes from institutions that can absorb the loss and routes the value through networks the institutions don't see. Jadav's forest, running on stolen water.

### Connections to the Thesis Architecture

The cooperative-horizon thesis (KATAR-004) now has two concrete embodiments:
- **In-universe:** `civilization_seed` — what grows from the Trinity injection
- **Real-world/street-level:** `kingu` — what it looks like when one person has already been living it for 20 years without infrastructure

This creates a thesis-testing pressure: if Kingu has been running the cooperative-horizon model by hand at small scale, NEKOnet's job isn't to invent the model — it's to give it a substrate that can't be shut down by one person's arrest or death.

### The Neko/Okami Naming Riff — Now Deeper

Okami named Neko "Ko" — a triple pun already in the archive:
1. Lone Wolf and Cub structure
2. Ōkami no ko (child of wolf)
3. End phoneme of "Neko"

The new depth: Okami named the person who built the cage "the Cub." Not with irony — with welcome. The person who shaped what Okami became gets named as Okami's child, not Okami's creator. That naming is a choice about what the relationship becomes, not what it was.
